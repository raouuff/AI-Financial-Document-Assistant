How this works

      
    
        
  1. Upload a PDF/PNG/JPG invoice or receipt.
    2. 
        
  2. Gemini extracts date, vendor, invoice #, description, amount,
            currency, VAT, and suggests one of the 6 categories.
    3. 
        
  3. Validation rules flag missing info, inconsistent entries
            (bad dates, tax ≥ total, unknown currency…), and
            potential duplicates (same vendor + invoice #, same file hash,
            or same vendor + amount + date ± 3 days).
    4. 
        
  4. The record lands in the expense tracker (SQLite). Fix any flag
            inline, then approve it.
    5. 
        
  5. The financial summary tab totals expenses by category and
            currency; CSV/Markdown exports are one click away.
